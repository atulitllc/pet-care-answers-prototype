Can cats drink milk?

🩺 Vet review pendingLast updated: Oct 13, 2026Reviewed: pending
⚠️ Not veterinary advice. If your pet is sick or hurt, contact a vet.
Short answer

Most adult cats do not need milk, and many have trouble digesting it. Fresh water is the best drink for cats.

🚩 When to see a vet now

  • Vomiting or diarrhea that continues
  • Refusing to eat or drink
  • Weakness or lethargy

In an emergency, call your vet or nearest emergency animal clinic.

🚨 Emergency: go to a vet now

  • Trouble breathing
  • Collapse
  • Seizures
  • Bloated belly or retching with nothing coming up
  • Suspected poisoning (chocolate, xylitol, lilies for cats, grapes)
  • Heavy bleeding
  • Male cat straining to pee
  • Pale gums

ASPCA Animal Poison Control: (888) 426-4435 (a fee may apply)

What is going on

Kittens drink their mother's milk, but many cats digest milk less well as they grow.

Cow's milk can lead to an upset stomach in some cats.

If you want a treat, ask your vet about cat appropriate options.

FAQ

Is kitten formula the same as cow's milk?

No. Orphaned kittens need a formula made for kittens; ask a vet for guidance.

How much water should my cat drink?

It varies. Tell your vet if you notice a big change in drinking.
