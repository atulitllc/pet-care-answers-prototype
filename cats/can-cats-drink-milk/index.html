Can cats drink milk?

By the Paws Answered editorial team · Published: Oct 13, 2026 ·🩺 Vet review pendingLast updated: Oct 13, 2026Reviewed: pending
⚠️ Not veterinary advice. If your pet is sick or hurt, contact a vet.
Short answer

It is best not to give it. The ASPCA explains that pets do not have much lactase, the enzyme that breaks down lactose, so milk and dairy can cause diarrhea or other stomach upset.

🚩 When to see a vet now

  • Vomiting or diarrhea that continues
  • Refusing to eat or drink
  • Weakness or lethargy

In an emergency, call your vet or nearest emergency animal clinic.

🚨 Emergency: go to a vet now

  • Trouble breathing
  • Collapse
  • Seizures
  • Bloated belly or retching with nothing coming up
  • Suspected poisoning (chocolate, xylitol, lilies for cats, grapes)
  • Heavy bleeding
  • Male cat straining to pee
  • Pale gums

ASPCA Animal Poison Control: (888) 426-4435 (a fee may apply)

What the sources say

Pets do not make significant amounts of lactase, the enzyme that digests lactose in milk. [1]

Because of that, milk and other dairy foods can cause diarrhea or other digestive upset. [1]

Fresh water is the drink cats need. Ask your vet before adding any new food or drink.

FAQ

What about cheese or yogurt?

The ASPCA groups milk and other dairy based products together as possible causes of diarrhea and digestive upset.

My cat drank some milk. Is that an emergency?

Usually not, but watch for diarrhea or vomiting and call your vet if it continues.
