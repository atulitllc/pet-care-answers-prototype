Why does my cat knead?

🩺 Vet review pendingLast updated: Oct 13, 2026Reviewed: pending
⚠️ Not veterinary advice. If your pet is sick or hurt, contact a vet.
Short answer

Kneading is a normal cat behavior often seen when a cat is relaxed or settling down. It is usually nothing to worry about.

🚩 When to see a vet now

  • Sudden behavior changes along with pain, limping, or hiding
  • Not eating or using the litter box

In an emergency, call your vet or nearest emergency animal clinic.

🚨 Emergency: go to a vet now

  • Trouble breathing
  • Collapse
  • Seizures
  • Bloated belly or retching with nothing coming up
  • Suspected poisoning (chocolate, xylitol, lilies for cats, grapes)
  • Heavy bleeding
  • Male cat straining to pee
  • Pale gums

ASPCA Animal Poison Control: (888) 426-4435 (a fee may apply)

What is going on

Kittens knead while nursing, and many cats keep the habit.

Some cats knead blankets or laps before lying down.

If claws hurt, a thick blanket on your lap can help.

FAQ

Should I stop my cat from kneading?

Usually there is no need. Gently redirect to a blanket if it is uncomfortable.
