Why is my cat so itchy?

By the Paws Answered editorial team · Published: Oct 13, 2026 ·Last updated: Oct 13, 2026Written from the cited sources. Not reviewed by a veterinarian.
⚠️ Not veterinary advice. If your pet is sick or hurt, contact a vet.
Short answer

Fleas are one of the most common reasons cats itch. Cornell says flea bites can cause intense itching, and constant scratching can open the skin to infection. Cats with flea allergy may get red, crusty bumps. Ask your vet about flea control, because some products can harm cats if used wrongly.

What the sources say

Cat fleas are tiny, from the size of a pinhead to about an eighth of an inch. [1]

Flea bites can cause intense itching, and a sensitive cat's constant scratching can open wounds that may become infected. [1]

Cats are bitten mostly on the back of the neck and at the base of the tail. Cats with flea allergy may get red, crusty bumps even where they have not scratched. [1]

Fleas can carry tapeworm eggs and bacteria, and heavy infestations can cause anemia in kittens. [1]

Treating the cat is not enough. Vacuum furniture, rugs and carpets carefully, and treat the home as well. [1]

Flea products can harm cats or people if misused, so use them only with your vet's guidance. [1]

🚩 When to see a vet

  • Scratching that causes sores, scabs or hair loss
  • Fleas on a kitten, or a kitten that seems weak or pale
  • Itching that continues after flea control

In an emergency, call your vet or nearest emergency animal clinic.

🚨 Emergency: go to a vet now

  • Trouble breathing
  • Collapse
  • Seizures
  • Bloated belly or retching with nothing coming up
  • Suspected poisoning (chocolate, xylitol, lilies for cats, grapes)
  • Heavy bleeding
  • Male cat straining to pee
  • Pale gums

FAQ

Can cat fleas affect people?

Yes. Cornell says cat fleas can carry germs that cause cat scratch disease, murine typhus and plague in people.

Why treat the house too?

Cornell says skipping the home lets fleas come back, so the problem never fully goes away.
