Why is my cat throwing up?

By the Paws Answered editorial team · Published: Oct 13, 2026 ·Last updated: Oct 13, 2026Written from the cited sources. Not reviewed by a veterinarian.
⚠️ Not veterinary advice. If your pet is sick or hurt, contact a vet.
Short answer

Occasional vomiting in an otherwise healthy cat, especially of hairballs, may not mean anything is wrong. Vomiting can also be a sign of many illnesses, so call your vet if it happens often or comes with other signs.

🚩 When to see a vet now

  • Vomiting repeatedly in one day, or for more than a day
  • Blood in vomit, or vomit that looks like coffee grounds
  • Lethargy, hiding, or refusing food
  • Signs of pain, a swollen belly, or straining to pee
  • Possible swallowed string, toy, plant, or medicine

In an emergency, call your vet or nearest emergency animal clinic.

🚨 Emergency: go to a vet now

  • Trouble breathing
  • Collapse
  • Seizures
  • Bloated belly or retching with nothing coming up
  • Suspected poisoning (chocolate, xylitol, lilies for cats, grapes)
  • Heavy bleeding
  • Male cat straining to pee
  • Pale gums

ASPCA Animal Poison Control: (888) 426-4435 (a fee may apply)

What the sources say

Cats vomit fairly readily. An occasional episode in a healthy cat, mainly of hair, may be nothing abnormal. [1]

Cats swallow hair when grooming. VCA says to contact your vet if your cat vomits hairballs more than once a month. [1]

Vomiting is a sign, not a diagnosis. It can come with infections, swallowed objects stuck in the gut, toxins, urinary blockage, pancreatitis, liver or kidney disease, thyroid disease, inflammatory bowel disease or cancer. [1]

Vomiting is different from coughing and from regurgitation. A video can help your vet tell them apart. [1]

FAQ

Is it normal for a cat to throw up hairballs?

Now and then, yes. VCA says more than once a month is a reason to contact your vet.

Should I stop feeding my cat after vomiting?

Ask your vet first. Vets usually guide feeding after vomiting, and cats should not go long without eating.
