Can dogs eat grapes?

By the Paws Answered editorial team · Published: Oct 13, 2026 ·🩺 Vet review pendingLast updated: Oct 13, 2026Reviewed: pending
⚠️ Not veterinary advice. If your pet is sick or hurt, contact a vet.
Short answer

No. Grapes, raisins and Zante currants can cause kidney failure in dogs, and there is no known safe amount. Call a vet or a pet poison hotline right away if your dog eats any.

🚩 When to see a vet now

  • Your dog ate any grapes, raisins, or foods containing them
  • Vomiting, diarrhea, or not eating
  • Weakness, lethargy, or drinking or peeing much more or less than usual

In an emergency, call your vet or nearest emergency animal clinic.

🚨 Emergency: go to a vet now

  • Trouble breathing
  • Collapse
  • Seizures
  • Bloated belly or retching with nothing coming up
  • Suspected poisoning (chocolate, xylitol, lilies for cats, grapes)
  • Heavy bleeding
  • Male cat straining to pee
  • Pale gums

ASPCA Animal Poison Control: (888) 426-4435 (a fee may apply)

What the sources say

Grapes, raisins and Zante currants can cause kidney failure in dogs. Cases have involved seedless and seeded, red and green, homegrown and store bought grapes. [2]

There is no well established toxic dose. Some dogs get sick after eating only a small amount. [2]

Hidden sources include trail mix, cereal, granola bars, baked goods and some jams. [2]

ASPCA Animal Poison Control lists grapes and raisins among foods to avoid, because they can lead to kidney damage. [1]

FAQ

What if my dog only ate one grape?

Call anyway. VCA notes there is no established safe amount and some dogs react to small amounts. Contact your vet or a pet poison helpline right away.

Are raisins in baked goods a problem?

Yes. VCA lists baked goods, cereal, trail mix and granola bars with raisins as possible sources.
