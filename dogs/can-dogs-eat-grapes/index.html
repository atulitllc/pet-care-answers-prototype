Can dogs eat grapes?

🩺 Vet review pendingLast updated: Oct 13, 2026Reviewed: pending
⚠️ Not veterinary advice. If your pet is sick or hurt, contact a vet.
Short answer

No. Grapes and raisins can be dangerous for dogs. Keep them out of reach, and contact a vet or a pet poison hotline right away if your dog eats any.

🚩 When to see a vet now

  • Your dog ate any grapes, raisins, or foods containing them
  • Vomiting, diarrhea, or not eating
  • Weakness, lethargy, or drinking or peeing much more or less than usual

In an emergency, call your vet or nearest emergency animal clinic.

🚨 Emergency: go to a vet now

  • Trouble breathing
  • Collapse
  • Seizures
  • Bloated belly or retching with nothing coming up
  • Suspected poisoning (chocolate, xylitol, lilies for cats, grapes)
  • Heavy bleeding
  • Male cat straining to pee
  • Pale gums

ASPCA Animal Poison Control: (888) 426-4435 (a fee may apply)

What is going on

Grapes and raisins are widely listed as toxic foods for dogs.

Do not wait for symptoms. Call a vet as soon as you know your dog ate them.

Do not try home remedies unless a vet tells you to.

FAQ

What if my dog only ate one grape?

Call your vet or a pet poison hotline anyway. They can advise you on next steps.

Are raisins in baked goods a problem?

Yes. Treat foods containing raisins the same as grapes.
