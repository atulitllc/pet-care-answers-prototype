Why is my dog eating grass?

By the Paws Answered editorial team · Published: Oct 13, 2026 ·🩺 Vet review pendingLast updated: Oct 13, 2026Reviewed: pending
⚠️ Not veterinary advice. If your pet is sick or hurt, contact a vet.
Short answer

Many dogs eat grass now and then. It is common, but check that the lawn is free of chemicals and talk to a vet if it happens a lot or comes with other symptoms.

🚩 When to see a vet now

  • Repeated vomiting or diarrhea
  • Not eating, lethargy, or signs of pain
  • Possible exposure to lawn chemicals or toxic plants

In an emergency, call your vet or nearest emergency animal clinic.

🚨 Emergency: go to a vet now

  • Trouble breathing
  • Collapse
  • Seizures
  • Bloated belly or retching with nothing coming up
  • Suspected poisoning (chocolate, xylitol, lilies for cats, grapes)
  • Heavy bleeding
  • Male cat straining to pee
  • Pale gums

ASPCA Animal Poison Control: (888) 426-4435 (a fee may apply)

What is going on

Some dogs seem to simply like the taste or texture.

Boredom can play a role, so walks and play may help.

Avoid areas treated with fertilizer or pesticides.

FAQ

Should I stop my dog eating grass?

If it is occasional and the grass is untreated, many owners do not. Ask your vet if you are unsure.
