How often should I bathe my dog?

By the Paws Answered editorial team · Published: Oct 13, 2026 ·Last updated: Oct 13, 2026Written from the cited sources. Not reviewed by a veterinarian.
⚠️ Not veterinary advice. If your pet is sick or hurt, contact a vet.
Short answer

Less often than you might think. The ASPCA says most dogs do not need a bath more than a few times a year. Regular brushing helps keep them clean in between. Before a bath, comb or cut out any mats, and rinse out all the soap so dirt does not stick to leftovers.

🚩 When to see a vet now

  • Red, flaky, or broken skin
  • Constant scratching, licking, or hair loss
  • A strong smell from ears or skin

In an emergency, call your vet or nearest emergency animal clinic.

🚨 Emergency: go to a vet now

  • Trouble breathing
  • Collapse
  • Seizures
  • Bloated belly or retching with nothing coming up
  • Suspected poisoning (chocolate, xylitol, lilies for cats, grapes)
  • Heavy bleeding
  • Male cat straining to pee
  • Pale gums

ASPCA Animal Poison Control: (888) 426-4435 (a fee may apply)

What the sources say

Most dogs do not need to be bathed more than a few times a year. [1]

Before a bath, comb or cut out mats from the coat. [1]

Rinse all soap out of the coat carefully, or dirt will stick to the leftover soap. [1]

Frequent brushing helps keep your dog clean and reduces shedding. [1]

If your dog has a skin problem or your vet prescribed a shampoo, follow your vet's instructions instead.

FAQ

Does brushing replace bathing?

Brushing does a lot of the work. The ASPCA recommends frequent brushing to keep dogs clean and cut shedding.

What if my dog smells or itches a lot?

Ask your vet. Odor and itching can be signs of a skin or ear problem.
