How often should I bathe my dog?

By the Paws Answered editorial team · Published: Oct 13, 2026 ·🩺 Vet review pendingLast updated: Oct 13, 2026Reviewed: pending
⚠️ Not veterinary advice. If your pet is sick or hurt, contact a vet.
Short answer

It depends on your dog's coat, skin, and lifestyle. Many dogs only need a bath when they are dirty or smelly. Your vet or groomer can suggest a routine.

🚩 When to see a vet now

  • Red, flaky, or broken skin
  • Constant scratching, licking, or hair loss
  • A strong smell from ears or skin

In an emergency, call your vet or nearest emergency animal clinic.

🚨 Emergency: go to a vet now

  • Trouble breathing
  • Collapse
  • Seizures
  • Bloated belly or retching with nothing coming up
  • Suspected poisoning (chocolate, xylitol, lilies for cats, grapes)
  • Heavy bleeding
  • Male cat straining to pee
  • Pale gums

ASPCA Animal Poison Control: (888) 426-4435 (a fee may apply)

What is going on

Use a shampoo made for dogs, not human shampoo.

Brushing between baths helps keep the coat clean.

Rinse well and dry thoroughly, especially thick coats.

FAQ

Can I use baby shampoo on my dog?

It is best to use a product made for dogs. Ask your vet for a recommendation.
